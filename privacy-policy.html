Privacy Policy for Lumi Drift

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lumi Drift handles information when you use the Lumi Drift mobile game.

Lumi Drift is an Android mobile game. The game does not require users to create a Lumi Drift account or provide a name, email address, telephone number, or other direct personal profile information in order to play.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Lumi Drift stores gameplay information locally on your device so that your progress can be preserved between sessions.

This may include information such as:

  • Game progress
  • Levels and progression
  • Scores and records
  • Virtual currencies and rewards
  • Hearts and gameplay resources
  • Achievements and missions
  • Unlocked cosmetic items
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Tutorial and onboarding progress

This gameplay save information is stored locally by Lumi Drift on the device.

2. Advertising

Lumi Drift uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising.

The currently supported advertising formats include:

  • App Open advertisements
  • Optional rewarded advertisements

Rewarded advertisements are optional. When a player chooses to watch an eligible rewarded advertisement and successfully earns the reward, Lumi Drift may provide an in-game reward such as a gameplay heart.

3. Information Processed by Google Mobile Ads

Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect or process information necessary for advertising, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

This may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general location of a device
  • User interactions with the app or advertisements, such as app launches, taps, and video views
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch performance, stability information, and SDK performance
  • Device or account identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID, App Set ID, and other applicable device identifiers

Google may use this information for purposes including advertising, advertising measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Lumi Drift uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) where applicable to request or manage advertising privacy choices.

Depending on your region and applicable law, you may be presented with privacy or consent choices before certain advertising data is used. These choices may affect whether personalized or non-personalized advertising is available.

You may also be able to reset or delete your Android Advertising ID through your Android device privacy settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Lumi Drift currently uses Google services for advertising and privacy management, including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

These services may process information according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Google Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

6. Data Sharing

Lumi Drift does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with Google as necessary to provide advertising, advertising measurement, analytics, security, consent management, and fraud-prevention services.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through Lumi Drift.

Google states that information transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using industry-standard transport encryption.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay progress stored locally by Lumi Drift generally remains on the device until it is overwritten, the application's data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google is retained according to the applicable third-party service's policies.

Lumi Drift currently does not require users to create a Lumi Drift account, so there is no Lumi Drift online user account that must be deleted.

For privacy questions or requests relating directly to Lumi Drift, please contact us using the address listed below.

9. Children's Privacy

Lumi Drift does not ask players to create an account or directly submit personal profile information in order to play.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been provided directly to us in a way that does not comply with applicable law, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Lumi Drift's features, third-party services, advertising systems, or legal requirements change.

When the policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed.
