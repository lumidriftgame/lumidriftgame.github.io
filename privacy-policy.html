Privacy Policy for Lumi Drift

Version 1.1

This policy describes how Lumi Drift handles information in version 1.1 of the Android game.

Age selection and information on your device

Lumi Drift asks you to choose an age range. The choices are Under 9, 9–12, 13–15, 16–17, 18+, and Prefer not to say. The game asks for an age range, rather than an exact date of birth. It stores the selected range and a record version on your device. If your age is unknown, including if you choose Prefer not to say, ordinary gameplay remains available. Your choice is separate from advertising consent.

The game stores progress, preferences, and gameplay statistics and diagnostics locally. Lumi Drift does not require you to create a Lumi Drift account to play. Version 1.1 does not enable Lumi Drift cloud saves. You can remove local game data through Android app settings or by uninstalling the app. Removing local data can also remove your saves.

Advertising and privacy choices

Version 1.1 allows advertising only after a saved 18+ age selection and the required privacy and consent checks. Lumi Drift does not initialize its Google Mobile Ads provider or request or show ads for players under 18 or of unknown age.

For eligible adult players, Lumi Drift uses Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded ads and show occasional interstitial ads. Rewarded ads grant the in-game bonus only when the advertising service confirms it was earned. App-open ads are disabled for all players in version 1.1. Ads and consent loading are not required for ordinary offline play.

Lumi Drift uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising privacy choices for eligible adult players where applicable. When available, open Settings → Privacy Options to review or change those choices. If ads are unavailable or cannot be requested under the current privacy state, ordinary gameplay remains available and the ad-dependent bonus may be unavailable.

When eligible advertising services are used, Google's SDK may collect and share device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available, IP address and the approximate location it can indicate, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, provide analytics, and prevent fraud. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. You can manage or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Google Play Games and online features

Eligible adult players may use Google Play Games for supported online features such as leaderboards. Google Play Games may process account and game activity information, including scores submitted to leaderboards. Local scores and ordinary gameplay remain available without signing in. The app restricts its Google Play Games access for players under 18 and of unknown age.

Data retention, deletion, and contact

Local game information remains on your device until removed. Information processed by Google through its services is subject to Google's privacy policy and the controls available through your Google account and Android device. Contact almog.ck@gmail.com with privacy questions, requests, or concerns involving a child's information.
